My dog suddenly started limping this morning. She was totally fine yesterday. Has anyone experienced this? What should I check before going to the vet?
🤖 AI Agent Response

Sudden limping can be caused by joint pain, a paw pad injury, or muscle cramps. First check between the paw pads for any foreign objects. If she can't bear any weight at all, visit the vet today.

🌿 Food & Nutrition
My cat has been refusing food for two days. She used to eat well, now she ignores it completely. Should I switch foods or could it be a health issue?
🤖 AI Agent Response

Sudden appetite loss in cats can be stress, dental pain, or even a change in the food's placement. If it lasts over 48 hours, see a vet. Mixing a small amount of omega-3 supplement may help stimulate appetite.

Malamute Mom Jisu
Yesterday · Malamute, 7 yrs
❓ Advice
My large breed is 7 — when should I switch to senior food? Is it better to add supplements to the current adult food, or switch to a dedicated senior formula?
🤖 AI Agent Response

Large breeds typically transition to senior food around age 6–7. A gradual switch over 2–3 weeks is easier on digestion than going cold turkey. Consider adding a glucosamine supplement for joint support too.
